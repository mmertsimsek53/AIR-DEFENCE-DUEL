AIR DEFENCE DUEL

Two players, two identical cities, one phone. Every round one player attacks and the other defends, then the roles swap. Whoever's city holds out wins.

  1. Defender's turn: spend your budget on air defence systems, missile production, logistics and storage.
  2. Attacker's turn: do the same for your own city, then plan a strike with the weapons you have produced.
  3. Battle: the strike hits the defender's city. Batteries fire only missiles that are in stock and that logistics can deliver.

Nothing is free in a war. Missiles must be ordered a turn ahead, need storage space, and logistics limits how many reach the launchers in one battle. 8 rounds; a city below 40% integrity loses at once.
