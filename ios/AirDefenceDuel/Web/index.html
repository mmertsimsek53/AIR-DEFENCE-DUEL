Missile Duel

Each side has a city with 6 targets. The sides take turns: one launches a missile from its silo and dives it onto an enemy target, the other launches one interceptor and detonates it near the missile. The closer the impact, the more damage. Destroy all 6 enemy targets to win.
Left: the joystick — slide it any way to turn, climb or dive. Right: faster, slower, and the big button — launch, then boost (attacking) or launch, then detonate (defending).
