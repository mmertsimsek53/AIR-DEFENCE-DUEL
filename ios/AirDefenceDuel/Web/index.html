AIR DEFENCE DUEL

Two cities. Build your air defences, strike your rival's city, defend live when they strike back. Every missile costs money.

  1. Setup: buy defence systems and place them on the pads, buy launchers, weapons and surveillance UAVs. Tap I'm done.
  2. Your turn: upgrade, plan a strike and press GO, or Done, no attack to save money. No clock in training.
  3. Under attack: tap a contact to make it the priority, long-press to hold fire on it.

Critical buildings are hidden until a UAV flies over them. Knock out the enemy's factory, power, radar or command centre to cripple them. A city at zero health is destroyed.
